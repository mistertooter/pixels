UNDER THE SURFACE

An RGB pixel stores red, green and blue channel values. Together, neighboring pixels form edges, texture and light.

What this illustrates: Prompt guidance, a noise-to-image metaphor, and a magnified RGB pixel view. Many diffusion systems refine a compressed latent representation and then decode it into pixels; they do not paint independent pixels one at a time.
